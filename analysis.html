What the week actually cost

Efficiency against the best eleven your own fifteen could have produced. The one call that decided it, named. Where you sat among every Clashd manager, not a global rank that means nothing to your league.

Forecasts you can check

Expected points for every player, built from public data: expected goals and assists, minutes, availability, defensive contributions and the strength of each opponent. Saved before every deadline and marked afterwards against what happened, alongside FPL's own numbers.
