What your group gets

Weekly head-to-head matches drawn between your members, divisions once you are big enough, and promotion and relegation at the end of each cycle.

Your call

You still run it

Rename the league, set what it plays for and remove anyone who should not be there. It is your league, with better furniture.

Two rules we will not bend

Privacy

Nobody is added without joining

Your league's standings are public information from FPL, but we do not keep them. For members who are not on Clashd we store only the public FPL entry id: no name, no team name, no score, nothing displayed and nobody contacted. If they have not joined in 30 days we delete it. You see a count, never a list.

Money

Bragging rights only

Clashd never collects, holds or moves money between your members. Whatever your group does among itself stays between you, and your FPL mini-league carries on untouched.
