Season pricing

Enter one, or enter everything

The pilot is free

Entry fees do not apply during the current pilot season. The pricing below is what the full season will cost when paid entry opens, and it will be confirmed to every registered player in advance.

Where the money goes

Pools grow with the players

Clashd does not promise a fixed prize and hope enough people enter. Each league pays out a published share of what that league actually collects.

1

You enter

Your payment is split across the leagues you chose. VAT is accounted for first, as required by UK law.

2

The pool fills

Seventy percent of every net entry goes straight into prize pools. The rest covers running Clashd. Each league shows its live pot in the app as players join.

3

Winners take their share

At the end of the competition the pool is paid out by the published split. Because prizes are a share of the pool, a league can never owe more than it holds.

Questions

The details

Can I add leagues later?

Yes. You pay the difference between your current tier and the one above it, at any point before the league you want to join has started.

What if I join late?

Season leagues are open until GW10, priced pro rata. Weekly and squad-selection leagues can be joined before any gameweek deadline.

How do I get paid?

SolvvSpace Ltd holds the pool and pays winners directly by bank transfer once the competition is settled and results are final.

Is this gambling?

No. Every Clashd competition is decided by a points-based grading system built on Fantasy Premier League performance. It is a game of skill, not a lottery. See the Terms.
