Questions

The details

Is it really free?

Yes. No entry fee, no deposit, no card details and no charge to claim a prize. SolvvSpace Ltd funds the prizes as an investment in building the community.

What if I join late?

Season leagues are open until GW10. Weekly and squad-selection competitions can be joined before any gameweek deadline, so you can start whenever you like.

How do I get paid?

SolvvSpace Ltd holds the pool and pays winners directly by bank transfer once the competition is settled and results are final.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no payment to enter and nothing at stake, and every competition is decided by a points-based grading system built on Fantasy Premier League performance. It is a game of skill, not a lottery. See the Terms.
