How it works

Simple to play.
Serious to win.

Clashd sits on top of the Fantasy Premier League game you already play. There's no second team, no manual scoring, and nothing new to learn. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

1

Link your FPL team

Go to fantasy.premierleague.com, open your profile, and copy the number after /entry/ in the URL. Paste it into Clashd once, and that's your team linked for the season.

2

Join your leagues

Your admin sends you an invite code. Enter it to register, then join any of the cash-prize leagues: season-long, weekly, or bonus challenges.

3

Scores sync automatically

Your official FPL points pull into Clashd every 30 minutes during active gameweeks. Leaderboards update live. You do nothing.

The engine

Your real FPL score, nothing invented

Clashd reads directly from the official Fantasy Premier League API. Whatever you score in FPL is exactly what you score in Clashd, including captains, chips, transfers, hits and all.

  • Points update every 30 minutes during gameweeks
  • Captain and Triple Captain counted automatically
  • Bench Boost, Free Hit and Wildcard all respected
  • Results confirmed once FPL marks the gameweek final

Prizes & fairness

Straight, transparent, above board

Clashd is built to be trusted. Here's how prizes and fair play work.

Skill, not chance

Every competition is decided by a points-based grading system built on your FPL performance. Outcomes reflect skill and knowledge, never luck. Clashd is a game of skill, not a lottery.

Transparent prize pools

Each competition's prize pool is the total of eligible entries, less any platform fee and taxes. Any fee is shown before you enter, no surprises.

One account, one manager

Invite-only and capped at 100 players. Multiple accounts or score manipulation means disqualification.

Prize payments & receivables

How the money works

Clear and above board. Here is exactly how entries and prizes flow.

1

You enter

Where a competition has an entry fee, you pay it to SolvvSpace Ltd, the operator. Your contribution joins that competition's prize pool. During the current pilot, entry is free.

2

We hold the pool

SolvvSpace Ltd holds and administers the prize pool for each competition. The pool is the total of eligible entries, less any disclosed platform fee and any taxes due. VAT is accounted for under UK law.

3

Winners are paid

When the competition ends, prizes are paid out by SolvvSpace Ltd to the qualifying participants, as decided by the points-based grading system. Prizes are paid only to verified players aged 18 or over.

Operated by SolvvSpace Ltd

All entries and payouts are processed through the company, one clear accountable operator.

Full detail is set out in the Terms & Conditions. Clashd is a skill-based competition for players aged 18+. Please play responsibly.
