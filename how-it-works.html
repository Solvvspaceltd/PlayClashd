How it works

Five minutes, then just play.

Clashd runs on the team you already manage. There is no second squad.
Step 1

Link your team

Enter your public FPL Team ID, or search for your team by name. That is the whole setup. We never ask for your FPL password and never sign in as you.

Step 2

Join what you fancy

Nine competitions, all free. Your place in Clashd Premier League is set up for you, with a division and a fixture list waiting.

Step 3

Play in FPL as normal

No second squad. Transfers and captain happen in the official game. The only thing you do here is name your Aside squads, and that locks at the FPL deadline.

Step 4

Let it score itself

Scores arrive from FPL through the gameweek. Clashd records your net score, after any transfer cost, because several competitions are built around exactly that.

Step 5

Climb, or hold on

Three points for a win. Two managers go up and two go down at the end of each cycle. Prizes are published before a competition opens and paid by bank transfer.

Clashd Premier League

Tiers open as the league grows. Below 20 managers it runs as Premier and Championship; at 20 the Elite tier unlocks with ten places.

TierWho is in it
EliteOpens at 20 managers, ten places
PremierTwo promoted, two relegated
ChampionshipNew managers start here
Timing

Promotion is not on a clock

It runs once every division in the league has settled its fixtures, so nobody goes up on a half-played season.

Joining late

Straight into the bottom tier

Fixtures are redrawn from the next gameweek, so you still get a full run of matches.

Scoring

Net, always

Your points after transfer costs. 42 with one hit is 38 here, which is the whole point of No Hit Squad and Transfer Genius.

Entry

Free, and 18+

No fee, no stake, no deposit. Prizes are funded by SolvvSpace. You can self-exclude at any time.

Data

Public data only

Everything comes from the public FPL service. Your FPL password is never asked for and never stored.
