Head-to-head fixtures are settled once that happens. We may correct a score, a table or a result if it was recorded wrongly, and our decision on scoring, fair play and prize eligibility is final.

8. Divisions, promotion and relegation

Clashd Premier League runs on divisions and weekly head-to-head fixtures. Divisions are sized and named by how many managers are in the league, and tiers open as it grows. Anyone joining part way through is placed in the lowest division and the remaining fixtures are redrawn from the next gameweek. Promotion and relegation run once every division in the league has settled all of its fixtures: two up and two down, unless we publish something different before a cycle begins.
