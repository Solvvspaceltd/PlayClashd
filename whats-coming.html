The roadmap

Built to grow

Clashd is launching lean and honest a tight app that does one thing well. Here's what's live today and the order we're building the rest. No vapourware, just the plan.

Live

The app

iOS & Android. Link FPL, join leagues, live leaderboards, invite-only.

Next

1v1 Battles

Head-to-head duels over a gameweek. Admin-created first, then self-serve.

Next

Web leaderboards

Check standings from any browser, no app needed.

Later

Account portal

Manage your profile, leagues, and prize history on the web.

In order

The build sequence

Roughly the order we'll ship things. Player demand can reshuffle it that's the point of starting small.

Shipped

The Clashd app & season leagues

The core product: link your FPL team, join seven cash-prize competitions, and follow live leaderboards. Invite-only, capped at 100 managers. This is live now.

Next up

1v1 Battles

Head-to-head competition, rolled out in phases starting with admin-created battles. The signature next feature — see the full plan.

Next up

Web leaderboards

A public web view of live standings so members and prospective players can follow the action without opening the app. Doubles as a shop window for the platform.

On the horizon

FPL insights & content

Gameweek reads, differentials, and tips content that sharpens your edge and pulls new managers to Clashd through search.

On the horizon

Mini-leagues for friends

Let members spin up private leagues for their own group of mates, within the same trusted, capped ecosystem.

Further out

Knockout cups & account portal

Bracket tournaments born from 1v1 Battles, plus a full web portal for managing your profile, leagues, and prize history in one place.
