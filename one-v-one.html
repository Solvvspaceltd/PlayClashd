The idea

Rivalry, settled on the pitch

Leagues are a marathon. Battles are a duel. Sometimes you just want to prove you're better than one specific person this is that.

Pick your format

A single gameweek for a quick settle, or a best-of series across several gameweeks for a proper grudge match.

Highest score wins

Both managers' official FPL points are tracked. When the gameweek goes final, the higher score takes the whole pot.

Track your record

Every battle counts toward your head-to-head record. Build a reputation as the manager nobody wants to draw.

Delivery approach

How we'll roll it out

We're shipping 1v1 in phases starting simple and admin-run, exactly how Clashd already works, then opening it up as demand proves out.

Phase 1 · Admin-created

Admin sets up the battle

The admin creates a 1v1 between two managers, picks the gameweek and the stake, and settles it when scores are final. Zero new complexity for players it works within the model Clashd already runs on today, and lets us prove people want it before building more.

Phase 2 · Challenge a rival

Players issue their own challenges

Any manager can challenge another in-app. The opponent accepts, the admin confirms the stake, and the battle goes live. Self-serve, but still with admin oversight on prizes.

Phase 3 · Automated & brackets

Knockout cups and auto-settlement

Battles settle automatically from the FPL API. Head-to-head records become a leaderboard. Single battles grow into knockout cups 8, 16, 32 managers in a bracket, last one standing takes the prize.

Why it works for Clashd

Small to build, big to play

1v1 fits the platform perfectly. It reuses the same FPL scores already flowing in, needs no new data, and starts as something the admin can run by hand.

  • Runs on the FPL scores Clashd already syncs
  • Starts admin-created low build, fast to test
  • Personal rivalry drives word-of-mouth invites
  • Grows naturally into cups and tournaments
