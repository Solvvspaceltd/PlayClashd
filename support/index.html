Support

One inbox, answered by the people who built it. admin@solvvspace.com

My score looks wrong

FPL pauses its data while matches are on, so scores can lag during a gameweek. Remember Clashd records your net score after transfer costs: 42 points with one hit is 38 here. If it still looks wrong once FPL has checked the gameweek, email us with your team name and the gameweek.

I cannot find my team

Search your team name in the app, or enter your FPL Team ID. You will find the ID on the official FPL site: open the Points tab and look at the number in the address bar. We never need your FPL password.

Changing my team name or badge

Your team name comes from FPL, so change it there and it follows. Your badge is chosen in Profile.

Notifications

Each kind can be turned on or off in Profile. If nothing arrives, check Clashd is allowed to send notifications in your phone's settings.

Anything else

Email admin@solvvspace.com. We are small and usually reply within a day.

Responsible play

Clashd is 18+ and every competition is free to enter, so you never stake your own money. You can self-exclude at any time in Profile. If taking part is becoming harmful, email us and consider contacting a responsible-gambling support organisation.
