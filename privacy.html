Clashd Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026 · A SolvvSpace Ltd product

This policy explains how Clashd, operated by SolvvSpace Ltd (Company No. 16153942, Bedford, England), collects and uses your information. Clashd is an 18+ skill-based fantasy football platform.

Data We Collect

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Fantasy Premier League Manager ID
  • Account creation date and league participation

We do not collect financial information. All prize payments are handled outside the app.

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your account and display your scores on leaderboards
  • To send notifications about results, ranks, and prizes
  • To provide admin oversight of the platform

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Railway infrastructure. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt. Session management uses secure JWT tokens.

FPL Data

Scores are sourced from the official Fantasy Premier League API. Clashd is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

Third Parties

We use Railway (infrastructure) and the official FPL API (score data). We do not sell or share your personal data with any third party for commercial purposes.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us.

Responsible Play

Clashd is for users aged 18 and over. You can request self-exclusion at any time in the app or by contacting us.
