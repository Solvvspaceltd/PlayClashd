The competitions

Seven ways to win

One FPL season, many prizes. Whether you're a set-and-forget manager or a transfer tactician, there's a competition built for how you play. Over £1,000 in prizes across the 2026/27 season.

Season

Clashd Season

The main event across all 38 gameweeks of the Premier League season.

£500 · £200 · £100
Your cumulative FPL score from GW1 to GW38 determines your final rank. No transfers or changes needed inside Clashd — manage your FPL team as normal and scores update automatically. Highest total at GW38 wins.
Weekly

Weekly Battle

Every gameweek is a fresh competition. Highest single-GW score wins the pot.

£50 winner takes all
Points hits are deducted as per your official FPL score. Chips count. Results confirmed once the FPL API marks the gameweek final, usually a few hours after the last match.
Bonus

Captain Royale

The manager with the highest cumulative captain points across the season wins.

£75 season prize
Your captain choice is pulled directly from your FPL team each gameweek. Triple Captain counts double. No input needed — just pick wisely. Winner announced at GW38.
Bonus

No Hit Squad

Finish the entire season without taking a single points hit.

£25 each for qualifiers
A hit is any transfer beyond your free transfer(s) in a gameweek. Wildcard and Free Hit don't count as hits. One hit across the season eliminates you. The pot is split equally between everyone who finishes hit-free.
Bonus

Transfer Genius

The manager whose transfers gained the most net points across the season wins.

£50 season prize
Transfer gain = points scored by players brought in minus those moved out, on the gameweek of each transfer. Calculated automatically from your FPL history. Highest cumulative gain at GW38 wins.
Bonus

Green Arrow

The manager who improves their overall FPL rank the most times across the season wins.

£50 season prize
A green arrow means your overall rank improved after a gameweek. We count how many you collect across the season. Most rank improvements at GW38 wins — consistency beats one big score.
Private

Elite Top 10

An invite-only private league for the top 10 ranked managers on the platform.

£100 · £40 · £10
Seeded at the start of the season based on previous performance. Same scoring rules as the main Season league. Final standings at GW38 decide prize distribution.
