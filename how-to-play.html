The rules

Updated 20 September 2026 · 2026/27 season

The Terms and conditions are the formal version. This is the readable one.

Who can play

Anyone aged 18 or over with a Fantasy Premier League team. One account per person. Registration is free and capped at the first 100 managers for the 2026/27 season.

Linking your team

You link with your public FPL Team ID, which you can find in the official game, or by searching for your team name in Clashd. We never ask for your FPL password.

How scores work

Your Clashd score for a gameweek is your official FPL points minus any transfer cost, so 42 points with one hit is recorded as 38. Scores update through the gameweek and are final once FPL marks the gameweek as checked, which is also when bonus points settle.

Deadlines

Clashd follows the official FPL deadline. Everything you do in the official game before it counts. The only thing you do inside Clashd is name your 5Aside and 7Aside squads, which lock at the same moment.

Head-to-head fixtures

In Clashd Premier League you are drawn against another manager each gameweek. The higher net score wins: three points for a win, one each for a draw. Tables are ordered on league points, then total points.

Divisions, promotion and relegation

Divisions form a pyramid named by size: Elite opens at 20 managers, with Premier and Championship below. Two go up and two come down at the end of each cycle, which runs once every division has settled all of its fixtures. Joining part way through puts you in the lowest division, with fixtures redrawn from the next gameweek.

Chips

Your FPL chips count exactly as they do in the official game. Clashd shows which chips you and your rivals still hold, but chips do not change how a Clashd competition is scored.

Prizes

Every competition is free to enter. The prize and how it is split are published before a competition opens. Prizes are paid by bank transfer to verified winners aged 18 or over, and must be claimed within 28 days of us contacting you.

Fair play

One account per person. No manipulating scores or standings, and no offensive team or league names. Breaking these rules can cost you your prize eligibility and your account. Our decision on scoring and fair play is final.

If something looks wrong

Scores can lag while FPL pauses its data during matches. If a score still looks wrong once the gameweek is checked, email admin@solvvspace.com with your team name and the gameweek.
